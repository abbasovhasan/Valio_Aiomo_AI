1. ENTER AS BUYER OR APPROVER

Open the account button and the "Sign in" dialog.

  - Select the "Buyer" card to enter the shared purchasing workspace.
  - Select the "Approver" card to enter its paired "Approvals" workspace.
  - No access code is required for these two cards.
  - "Remember me for 30 days on this device" is optional for either role.

Quick roles exist only when the server enables the controlled demo. If disabled,
standard account sign-in remains available; the browser must not grant itself a
role. The shared quick-demo pair is separate from private accounts and the old
Aurora demonstration. Do not put personal or confidential information in it.

Use "Switch role", or reopen the account dialog, to change perspective. Check
that the displayed role matches the workspace and allowed actions. Buyer can
edit purchasing requests; Approver reviews exact requests and cannot buy.

Reload in each role. The correct identity should recover. Sign out, reload and
try a protected action: it should require sign-in. Rapidly switching roles or
closing/reopening sign-in must not restore the previous role's permissions.


2. KEEP STANDARD AUTHENTICATION AVAILABLE

Choose "Use an account" for these secondary options:

  "Account sign-in" - your existing username and password.
  "Create workspace" - name, username, 12-128-character password and the private
                       buyer invitation code; no email is required.
  "Access code"      - the original buyer/approver code path.

Ctrl+H shows hidden folders. Copy a code only into the website; never paste it
into chat, screenshots or this guide. Deployment codes can differ from the local
file. A workspace ID alone must grant no permissions.

For a private buyer's approval, use "Approve this workspace", then the separate
manager access code with the intended workspace ID. The public Approver card
opens only the quick-demo workspace, not another buyer's private records.

Test Remember Me checked and unchecked. Checked uses a persistent 30-day cookie;
unchecked uses a browser-session cookie and an eight-hour server expiry. Browser
session restoration can retain cookies. "Sign out" explicitly revokes access.


3. START A SMALL ORDER WITH DELIVERY AND BUDGET

Enter as Buyer. Set "Delivery date" within the next 30 days and optionally
"Maximum budget (€)" before ordering. Try:

  I need 8 kg of mozzarella and 12 kg of cheddar.

Click "Build cart". Check that both requirements have their own quantities and
units. Choose eligible actual products where a decision is required. Prices and
stock must use the displayed source; "Requires quote" never means zero.

Try a conversational update, such as reducing only one item. The other item,
its selection, hard restrictions and the delivery date should remain intact.
Use quantity "Update", replace a product and remove a request. Totals and
package surplus should update. A missing product or unsupported conversion
must produce an honest question or blocker.

Use a low requested budget. A permitted exact budget exception can be requested
explicitly in Review order; it must not silently raise your original budget. Date changes must update quotation and final review;
unknown or later availability must not become a promised delivery.

The cart summarizes requested delivery and maximum budget. "Edit requirements"
returns to the authoritative request controls; there should not be a second
conflicting date/budget editor in the cart. A purchasing-policy limit is separate
from your item budget: increasing that budget cannot grant more authority or
replenish the workspace's simulated spending allowance.


4. SEARCH DIFFERENT REAL PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Open "Products". Try cheese, a different available food family and a nonfood
foodservice item. Also try mixed capitalization, a partial name and an actual
SKU. Check the total and use Next/Previous. Matching inventory must not collapse
into one arbitrary recommendation.

Open "Filters" for category, brand, required origin or a verified dietary claim.
Remove an active filter or reset all. Try an absent product name and confirm a
clear empty state. Category codes are supplied without translated category names.

Try Sort: "Best match", "Name · A to Z" and "Name · Z to A" cover the filtered
catalog. Price per sales unit/kg/L and earliest available date explicitly apply
to "this page", since the whole catalog has not been quoted. Unknown values
must stay last, not become zero. "Available on this quoted page" narrows only the
currently quoted page; the catalog match count should remain distinguishable.

Products has its own "Use saved optional preferences while browsing" control.
Change a Products filter/sort, then return to Smart order: its filters must not
have changed. Repeat in the other direction. Saved mandatory food restrictions
remain global, and the selected cart must survive either page's browsing changes.

Click "Select", enter quantity/unit and inspect the quotation, then "Add to cart".
The selection should remain editable even when pricing needs a quote, while
confirmation stays blocked until commercial facts are verified.

The owner has source-derived examples in .local/role-demo-cases.json: a 75-cl
syrup, measured-weight KG product, milk alternatives and two additional actual
categories. These private examples are for checking the real catalog, not a
fixed product list built into the application.


5. UNDERSTAND PACKAGES, MEASURED WEIGHT AND PRICES

Where verified volume exists, look for a concise calculation such as:

  2 sales packages × 0.75 L = 1.5 L ordered for 1 L requested.

Check package price, price per litre/kg, total and surplus against the same
quantity basis. Whole fixed packages must respect the supplier's lot increment.
For measured KG sales, fractional weights are valid where metadata supports
them: 1.5 kg with a 0.01-kg increment should stay 1.5 kg. A 1.2-kg increment
rounds a 1.5-kg request up to 2.4 kg, not six whole kilograms.

Internal codes such as KI have no supplied translation. They belong in Sources,
not an invented "case" label. Kilograms ordered are not fractional packages.
"Consumer units" do not automatically mean individual contents of a multipack.
Missing/contradictory conversions, variable weight and unsupported charges must
remain explicit rather than being guessed. Litres and kilograms are not
interchangeable without independent metadata for each dimension.

Item subtotals include tax. Delivery/service charges remain excluded where
unknown; availability is not a reservation.


6. COMPARE ANY TWO OR THREE AND OPEN DETAILS

On Smart order or Products, tick "Compare" on any two actual products. Selecting
must not immediately open a dialog or change the cart. Click "Compare items" beside the alternatives count to open it. Products shows
"Compare items (2)" in its selection bar; add a third to compare three. Products can come from different
categories or later searches/pages. A fourth should offer explicit "Replace"
choices and Cancel rather than silently dropping an earlier choice.

Inspect aligned quantities, sales-unit price, valid price/kg or price/L, stock,
date, brand, origin, nutrition basis, ingredients/allergens and unknown fields.
Different categories need not be substitutes. No kg-to-litre density assumption
or a supposed cheapest product across an unquoted catalog is acceptable.
"Refresh quotes" should retain the compared products while updating evidence.

Use "Remove", "Change", per-item "Details" and "Clear comparison". Smart order's
"Choose for this request" changes only that requirement; Products' "Add to cart"
adds the preferred item. The comparison list itself is independent of the cart,
and each page keeps its own list. No comparison controls belong in History or
the Meal planner. Current source assets have no supplied product photos: a
neutral missing-image treatment is honest, not a picture of an invented product.

Display several alternatives ("Show N more alternatives" expands a shortened
list). Note their positions and selected state. Click "Product details" on one
product.

The shared "Product details" drawer should slide in from the right on desktop;
on a phone it opens as a bottom sheet. Its compact header shows Product details
and SKU; the full product name appears once in the scrollable body. Cards must
keep their layout, without a very tall card or an empty column. Inspect overview,
purchasing, specifications, ingredients/allergens and Sources & package evidence.

Check supplied brand, SKU, physical size, requested/rounded quantities, price,
availability/date, manufacturer, origin, claims, storage and evidence. Missing
facts must remain unknown. Positive allergens, may-contain statements and explicit
free-from declarations are different. Lactose-free is not milk-allergy safe.

Scroll the drawer's content, and use Tab/Shift+Tab, the close button, Escape and
the backdrop to check native modal behavior. Focus should return predictably;
comparison position/choices and cart contents/totals must not change merely from
opening details. The independent Compare dialog keeps its own layout. Repeat on
a phone-sized viewport and confirm the sheet stays usable above the screen edge.

Try sorting and filtering alternatives. Unknown fields must not become verified
matches. The chosen product should remain visible or pinned, even outside a
filter. Repeated Choose actions and reload must not duplicate search controls.


7. SAVE MEANINGFUL RESTRICTIONS AND OPTIONAL PREFERENCES

Open "Buying preferences". Use "Mandatory food requirements" for a hard diet or
allergen exclusion; try No pork and a peanut exclusion separately. Save and
search/build again. Known violations and unknown safety must not appear safe.
Mandatory restrictions remain enforced when optional defaults are unchecked.

Optional brand/origin/organic/priority choices guide results; they do not create
missing certifications or alter spending authority. Explicit request rules take
precedence. Clear a saved-only restriction, re-quote and check it clears from the
same cart; an explicit restriction in the request must remain.


8. PASS AN EXACT REQUEST BETWEEN BUYER AND APPROVER

Use two separate browsers/browser profiles for a buyer and independent manager.
Switching roles in the buyer's browser must NOT authorize its own request.
The shared quick Buyer/Approver pair is useful for a demo; private buyers use
"Approve this workspace" and the separately held manager code in a second
browser. A workspace ID alone grants no authority. Shared codes simulate actors,
not real enterprise employee identity across devices.

Create a valid basket above its requested budget or buyer authority, within all
hard limits. With authored installation defaults, buyer authority is €1,400,
per-order ceiling €2,500 and lifetime workspace simulated allowance €6,000.
These are simulator settings, not official Valio limits or supplier bank funds.
Raising requested budget must not increase authority or replenish allowance.

Choose "Review order". Inspect original request, chosen products, requested and
rounded quantities, source, delivery, dietary evidence and cost scope. Expand
"Workspace policy & allowance" for cumulative details. Opening review does not
re-quote or submit. Back to cart must retain unsent text, products, filters,
sorting and optional comparisons.

Example: original requested budget €800, verified basket €836. Review should
show exactly €36 extra, retain €800, and offer "Request approval" when permitted.
A short explanation is optional. Only clicking that action creates a pending
queue entry. Direct confirmation remains unavailable. Financial approval cannot
bypass missing stock, dietary evidence, invalid conversion or hard allowance.

In the separate eligible Approver browser, open that request from Approvals.
Check buyer/workspace, title, submission time, complete basket, €800/€836/€36,
explanation and exact reasons. Read-only comparison must not change the basket.
Try "Request changes" with a note, then Buyer "Refresh status"; edit/re-quote
and request fresh approval. "Decline basket" records rejection and requires a
fresh buyer revision. "Approve exact basket" approves only the reviewed version.

Approval must not create an order or increase future authority/budgets. The
buyer refreshes status, reviews Approved, then decides whether to confirm the
simulation. Product, quantity, date, price, policy or mandatory-rule changes must
invalidate/stop stale approval. Expired quotes require buyer refresh. Authorized
change requests/declines remain available for recovery from hard blockers.

This scenario can stop after approval. No high-value confirmation is necessary.
Pending approval reserves no funds or stock. Competing submissions consume the
same lifetime allowance atomically; a later basket can be blocked. Re-entering
or switching roles must never reset spending or audit history.


9. REVIEW ONE SMALL SIMULATION ONLY WHEN DESIRED

For a complete permitted small cart, choose "Review order" and inspect its
exact items, quantities, date and subtotal. "Confirm simulated order" records a
simulation only. Repeated requests with the same key must not create duplicates.

Inspect the generated receipt/reference and "Verify receipt". Verification is
read-only; reload should recover the same saved result. This is gateway-record
consistency, not a supplier confirmation, invoice or proof of delivery. Repeated
simulations consume the workspace's synthetic spending allowance.


10. CHECK TYPE, VOICE, IMAGE AND CAMERA

  "Type a message" - focus and edit the request.
  "Voice input"    - allow microphone, speak, Stop & transcribe; Cancel discards.
  "Upload image"   - select an existing JPEG/PNG shopping-list image.
  "Take a photo"   - native phone camera or desktop preview/Capture photo.

Voice stays bounded to 30 seconds. Original images must be at most 5 MiB; normal
high-resolution photos under that size are normalized before secure processing.
A larger original must be rejected clearly without a stuck loading state.

Review and correct "Editable extracted text" before "Add to request". Adding
must preserve existing typed text/date/budget, and Build cart must wait until
reviewed text is actually added. Discard/Cancel must leave the request usable.
There is no audio-file upload option; live microphone processing is preserved.

Image reading is automatic using the configured NVIDIA reader; no provider menu
or manual cloud checkbox is needed. Use nonconfidential demonstration images.
Any offline fallback must name its actual source honestly. Voice uses local
speech recognition. Permission denial, absent hardware, corrupt files and camera
closure should offer useful typing/upload recovery and stop unused media streams.


11. REOPEN HISTORY AND CUSTOMIZE A MEAL PLAN

In "History", open a request, reuse it as a fresh draft, archive/restore it and
remove a disposable entry. Reload to check persistence. Removal hides personal
history metadata; recorded orders and required audit evidence remain retained.
Different private accounts must not see each other's entries.
Buying preferences should not appear inside History.

Open "Meal planner" / "Plan a meal". Start with "Meal name" and "Servings".
Spaghetti bolognese is an explicitly illustrative, unverified example; another
dish should start an editable custom list, without inventing a verified recipe.
Expand "Dietary requirements" or "Ingredient preferences" when needed.

Generate the plan and inspect "Your ingredients". Use "Add ingredient", edit an
ingredient search, grams per serving and "Use for", or remove a row. Recalculate
after changes; dirty quantities must not transfer as an unchanged plan. Selections are not displayed as compliant while requirements are dirty. After
recalculation, only a still-eligible selected SKU may survive through its
ingredient/cohort ID. Incompatible, missing-evidence and invalid-conversion
choices must stay disabled/unselected; unresolved rows say "No verified suitable
product selected". A product name alone never verifies dietary suitability.

For the example, try 40 servings, 8 vegetarian, 4 gluten-free and 2 needing both.
Expected groups: 30 /2 /6 /2, counted once each. Check exact displayed kilograms.
Expand catalog choices when desired; quantity calculations alone do not prove
price, stock, dietary suitability or recipe safety.

Choose "Add ingredients to cart" and inspect "Delivery, budget & pricing".
If another cart exists, require the explicit handoff decision before replacing
it. Exact ingredients, restrictions, date and budget should reach a separate
Smart order draft. Fresh chef acknowledgement and unknown hard dietary evidence
must still block unsafe confirmation. This step creates a draft, not an order.

"Demo tools" retains Data & model settings, Trust scenarios, Decision recorder,
Gateway API and About trust. The old Aurora trust lab is a separate mandate;
quick-demo/private accounts must not silently acquire it. Standard owner-issued
access codes are still available where that legacy demonstration is intended.


12. FINISH THE DESKTOP, MOBILE AND KEYBOARD REVIEW

Check major screens at 320, 375, 390 and 430 px, then tablet/desktop. Inspect
the A4TB header/home link and compact browser icon, sign-in/registration, Buyer,
Approver, Products, product details, preferences,
History, meal planner, cart and review/result. On a narrow screen, use the mobile
Cart control; it should open a usable dialog instead of a cramped sidebar.

Look for overflow, clipped text/buttons, touch-target problems, stuck loading,
misaligned cards and missing selection/error feedback. Use Tab/Enter/Space and
Escape; focus should remain visible and dialogs should close predictably. A
viewport check alone does not prove behavior with every phone's real keyboard.

Record request, role, product/unit, button, expected result and actual result for
any issue. Screenshots must exclude passwords, codes and private customer data.
Do not submit the Junction entry, purchase anything or activate paid resources.
