## Resulting implementation

- **Controlled demo roles:** explicit server feature flag, fixed isolated paired Buyer/Approver workspace, normal revocable sessions, Remember Me, scoped approval queue and exact-fingerprint approve/decline with reasons. Standard account creation/login/access-code paths remain available. Client-supplied roles/workspaces cannot grant authority. Frontend session restoration and role changes use authoritative backend identity and reject stale async responses.
- **Compact, useful product details:** one shared native modal right drawer on desktop and bottom sheet at mobile widths, compact Product details/SKU header, full name once in the scrollable body, keyboard/Escape/backdrop/focus behavior and unchanged card geometry. Cart and Compare keep independent layouts. Purchasing/specification/ingredient/allergen/source sections and mandatory warnings remain intact. Normalized evidence retains origin/claims/organic facts, declared nutrition basis/precision/zero, storage temperatures and physical conversion evidence. Supplier media IDs are not image files: the catalog gets an honest neutral fallback rather than invented product photographs or URLs.
- **Generic quantities:** actual consumer-level CLT converts to litres; fixed KG-sale/KG-base quantities obey fractional source lot increments. ST/KI fixed commercial packs still round to whole lot-compatible packages. Mass/volume and commercial price basis remain distinct; missing/conflicting facts, variable weight and unsupported contained-piece counts stay explicit. No KI-to-case or kg-to-litre guess was introduced.
- **Independent workspaces and comparison:** Smart order and Products own their filtering and manual comparison lists; saved mandatory food restrictions remain shared intentionally. Explicit Compare (2)/(3), maximum-three replacement, removal/change/details and per-product choose/add are separate from cart selection. Details do not implicitly choose products. Comparison preserves unknowns and physical unit dimensions.
- **Discovery and pricing scope:** name sorting applies before pagination over the entire filtered catalog. Sales-unit/kg/litre price and availability-date sorting applies to the bounded quoted page, labelled accordingly, with unknowns last in either direction. Filters use real source facts; optional browsing preferences have their own default-off control. No full-catalog cheapest-price claim or density inference is made.
- **One cart editor:** delivery and budget have one authoritative request editor; the cart summarizes them and offers Edit requirements. A customer budget cannot enlarge purchasing authority or refill cumulative allowance. History has no Buying preferences panel; underlying purchasing evidence remains retained when personal history metadata is hidden.
- **Custom meal planning:** clear dish/servings entry, optional dietary/ingredient preferences, editable add/remove/replace/grams-per-serving/use-for ingredients, recalculation and dirty-transfer protection. Stable ingredient/cohort identifiers preserve valid choices. Illustrative defaults are declared; arbitrary dishes start editable custom ingredients. Existing-cart handoff requires an explicit decision before a separate draft opens through the shared purchasing engine, retaining date/budget/restrictions and chef review.

The details interaction is a web adaptation of Apple's [Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets) and [Modality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/modality) guidance: one focused surface, an obvious close action and return to the original context. The requested desktop drawer and mobile sheet share the same component.

The initial public browser navigation reused cached B4 HTML after the server published Bj9; reloading loaded Bj9. HTML entry documents now explicitly require revalidation (`no-cache, max-age=0, must-revalidate`), while fingerprinted JS/CSS retain their existing file behavior. Eight static-shell regressions cover direct/deep entry, changed bundle references and API/asset boundaries. This addresses both the previously unpublished redesign and stale entry-document reuse.

## Financial findings and correction

Independent raw source evidence establishes 0.5kg per sales unit for the screenshot's 500g unsalted butter and 1L per sales unit for its UHT whole milk. Thus **200kg→400 sales units** and **300L→300 sales units** are correct.

The screenshot exactly matches authored fixtures: **400×€8.91 +300×€3.87 =€4,725**. A €101,000 customer budget leaves €96,275. A separate configured €2,500 per-order purchasing-policy limit still blocks that basket; the €6,000 cumulative allowance is a different rule depending on prior spend. Neither policy was enlarged, reset or bypassed. The UI now reports the distinct reason and does not offer a budget increase as a policy fix.

A separately authorized, bounded two-product supplier read on 15 November for 17 November returned current sandbox tax-inclusive sales prices of €3.75 and €1.86: **€1,500 +€558 =€2,058**. This is dated customer/sandbox-context evidence, not the historical screenshot price or a future commercial promise. Supplier comparison-price rounding does not replace sales-unit arithmetic. Price validity, cutoff, availability, approval and final freshness remain independent checks; no reservation or order was made by this audit.

The authored fixture changed gross prices without updating its net-price evidence. A narrow correction now derives net from the stable authored gross and the row's declared tax rate; legacy fixture prices remain unchanged. Order arithmetic continues to use Decimal tax-inclusive sales-unit values and explicit item-price scope.
