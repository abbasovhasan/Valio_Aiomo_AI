1. OPEN THE WEBSITE AND SIGN IN

Open the website above. On a phone, open the navigation menu if needed.
Choose "Smart order".

Click the upper-right profile button, labelled "Account and sign-in" for
assistive technology. Before sign-in it shows "Open your desk". The window is called
"Sign in". Enter the private buyer code in "ACCESS CODE", then
click "Open this session". The profile should now identify the buyer role.

Where to find the codes privately:

  The project owner can open this file on the prepared computer:
  /home/hasan/Documents/ChatGPT/Valio_Aiomo_AI/.local/access.json

  The file contains a "buyer" value and a separate "approver" value. In the
  file manager, Ctrl+H shows hidden folders such as .local. Copy only the
  required value into the website's password field. Do not paste either code
  into chat, this guide, screenshots or a public document.

  A deployed installation may have separately configured codes. If the local
  code is not accepted, ask the project owner for the current deployment's
  buyer or approver code through an appropriate private channel.

You can also create your own private demonstration workspace:

  - In the sign-in window, choose "Create workspace".
  - Enter "Your name", a "Username" (3-40 supported characters) and a
    "Password" of 12-128 characters.
  - Enter the installation's private buyer code in "Buyer invitation code".
  - Click "Create my workspace". No email address is required.
  - Later, use "Account sign-in", your username/password and "Sign in".

The invitation creates an isolated demonstration workspace. It is not a
Valio Aimo customer registration or contract. Your saved preferences, cart,
simulated spending and receipts belong to that workspace. Two accounts must
not see or change each other's private records.

"Remember me for 30 days on this device" is optional. With it checked, the
browser receives a persistent sign-in cookie with a 30-day server expiry.
Without it, the cookie is for the browser session and the server expiry is
8 hours. Browser session-restore settings can retain session cookies; use
"Sign out" to revoke the session explicitly. Neither choice saves your
password in this application's local storage.

The buyer creates and edits carts and confirms permitted simulated orders.
The approver can approve an exact eligible cart but cannot act as its buyer.
Changing a label or describing yourself as a manager does not grant that role.

If an old cart reopens, click "New request" before beginning a fresh example.
This starts a new cart; it does not erase previously recorded receipts.


2. START WITH A SMALL MIXED ORDER

For a predictable first check, enter:

  I need 20 litres of lactose-free milk and 10 kg of cheese delivered by
  Monday. Choose the cheapest suitable products.

Set "Delivery date" before ordering and optionally enter "Maximum budget (€)".
Enter the date first, then the budget: the date must stay visible and both
values must reach the resulting cart. Later changes can be applied from the
early controls using "Apply", or in the cart using "Apply requirements".
Click "Build cart". Ordinary customer ordering does not require choosing an AI
provider or debugging source. Actual quotations may change; inspect their source.
If the owner enabled optional "Demo settings", an authored fixture can be used
for a repeatable check. Fixture prices must remain labelled as demonstration data.

Look for these results:

  - Milk and cheese appear as two separate requests.
  - The milk retains its lactose-free requirement.
  - Product cards show actual catalog names and SKU numbers.
  - Each card shows the sales package, order quantity, quantity after rounding,
    sales-unit price and line total.
  - A selected card has a "Selected" mark and an explanation of the selection.
    A selected request with a constraint problem must still say "Needs review"
    and retain the actual warning; selection alone does not mean it is valid.
  - The right-hand shopping cart contains the selected products.
  - The quotation source is labelled accurately; demo prices are not live prices.

The system may ask you to "Choose" a suitable product. Click a valid candidate
rather than guessing its name in a message. A blocked candidate must explain
why it cannot be chosen.

The fixture prices and stock are authored test values. The product identities
and specifications come from the supplied challenge catalog. "Cheapest" means
lowest rounded item subtotal among the retrieved compliant candidates; it is
not a guarantee of the cheapest possible basket across the entire catalog.

Next, if useful, try the "Stock the breakfast kitchen" example. It requests
200 litres of lactose-free milk, 10 kg of butter and 5 kg of cheese. Check all
three groups. An inconsistent or missing package conversion should produce a
visible issue, not an invented quantity or a silently accepted huge package.


2A. SEARCH THE PRODUCTS WORKSPACE

Open "Products". Set a delivery date and optional budget before adding items.
Search for "cheese" in "Search products". Check the result count and use
"Next"/"Previous" to inspect additional pages; a matching category must not
collapse into an arbitrary single recommendation.

Try a partial product name, different capitalization, a real SKU, a category
and "Filters" for brand/origin/verified dietary claims. Reset the filters and
try an absent name: it should show "No matching products", without inventing any.

Active filters stay visible as removable chips even when "Filters" is closed;
its heading shows the filter count. Use the visible "Reset filters" before
trying a different product category. Saved dietary and ingredient exclusions
appear under "Mandatory food requirements applied" and remain active after
this reset. Manage them deliberately in "Buying preferences".

Move to "Smart order" and back to "Products". The search, filter, selected
product and page should remain where you left them.

Also try a real catalog product with a long full name. Select it and make a
follow-up change; the name must not be cut off or rejected. The per-request
"Product search" field accepts up to 200 characters.

Click "Select" on a product. Inspect its package and "Product details".
Set "Quantity" and "Unit": sales packages, kg required, litres required or
pieces required. Check the rounded sales-package count and total, then click
"Add to cart". Change quantity, replace the product or remove it from the cart.
Other selected products should stay intact. A missing price should say
"Requires quote"; it is not zero. Eligible products can remain in a draft,
while confirmation requires verified prices, availability and hard restrictions.

Under "Buying preferences" -> "Mandatory food requirements", try "No pork"
and a peanut exclusion separately. Save, search again and inspect alternatives.
Known violations and unknown compliance must never appear verified safe.
Mandatory restrictions remain enforced when optional preferences are off.
Clear test restrictions deliberately before starting an unrelated example.


2B. REOPEN, REUSE AND ORGANIZE HISTORY

Create a request, then open "History". Click its title to reopen it and check
the saved quantities and choices. "Reuse" should create a separate draft;
review its date, budget and fresh quotation before considering confirmation.

Click "Archive", enable "Show archived requests", and use "Restore". Reload
to check persistence. Use the remove icon and confirm "Remove from history"
on a disposable example. It should leave your personal list, while recorded
orders and required audit evidence remain retained. Another buyer workspace
must not gain access to these entries.


3. CHECK QUANTITIES AND PACKAGE ROUNDING

Under a requested product, inspect "Required quantity". Change the number and
click "Update". The product quotation and cart should be recalculated.

You can also make a conversational change. In the request box enter:

  Reduce the cheese to 5 kg.

Click "Update cart". The cheese quantity should change while the milk
quantity, lactose-free rule and unrelated selections remain intact.

For the breakfast example, try:

  Reduce the butter to 5 kg.

Where whole packs supply more than requested, look for "extra from whole
packs". The cart should show requested and ordered quantities and the surplus.
Do not assume litres and kilograms are interchangeable.

If the same product supplies more than one recipe group, the purchasing cart
must consolidate that product before rounding. The final cart quantities are
the authoritative shopping quantities; do not add separate candidate estimates
together yourself.


4. COMPARE AN ALTERNATIVE OR REFINE A PRODUCT SEARCH

Open "Product evidence" on a candidate. Where supplied, it shows the brand,
manufacturer, declared origin, dietary/organic evidence, ingredients, allergen
declarations and storage instructions. It also shows package evidence, available
quantity, the estimated available date, historical delivered quantity, catalog
source and catalog update time.

Unknown information should remain unknown. A catalog update date is not proof
of current stock. Historical delivered quantity is not an on-time guarantee.
Allergen supplier codes may remain visible when their meaning is not supplied;
missing declarations must not be presented as proof that an allergen is absent.
Open "How the package conversion is supported" to inspect its recorded basis.
A category-average carbon estimate, where shown, is not a measured product
footprint or a measured saving.

Open "Sort & filter products" on an ingredient request. Try "Sort by":

  - "Suggested order".
  - "Item subtotal - lowest first".
  - "Price per [requested unit] - lowest first".
  - "Brand - A to Z".
  - "Declared origin - A to Z".
  - "Available date - earliest first".

The actual UI uses a separator between each label's two parts. Unknown prices,
brands, origins or dates sort after known values. The selected product remains
pinned so it is not lost when you sort.

Try "Brand", "Origin includes", "Declared attributes" and
"Compliant choices only". Attribute filters require explicit supplied claims;
unknown does not mean a match. Check the "X of Y products match" count. If the
selected product falls outside the filter, it must remain visible with a
notice. "Reset view" clears the view settings. No result should explain the
empty filter and offer a reset, rather than silently change your order.

The first four candidates are shown initially. "Compare N more products"
opens the rest. Every returned product should remain inspectable and, when
valid, selectable. Sorting and filtering only change the view; they do not
relax hard constraints or change your cart by themselves.

Prices and recommendations cover the retrieved candidates and rounded
quantities. They are not proof of the cheapest possible basket across the
entire catalog. A "Basket change" figure is meaningful only when the server
supplies a valid full-basket comparison; do not invent a difference otherwise.

Click "Choose" on another suitable candidate. Check that the cart, price and
selected mark update without removing the other requested products.

To search for a more suitable product, edit "Product search" on the relevant
request and click "Find products". For example, refine a broad cheese search
to "cheddar".

Expected result: only that product search changes. Its required quantity and
hard dietary/origin requirements stay in place. A cheaper substitute must not
silently waive a hard requirement.

To remove an item deliberately, use its bin icon ("Remove" followed by the
product request). The cart should update and unrelated items should remain.


4A. SAVE YOUR USUAL BUYING PREFERENCES

Open "Buying preferences" below the request composer while signed in as buyer.
Try a "Purchasing priority": balanced comparison, lowest rounded item cost,
manual quality-specification review, preferred origin, or earliest verified
availability. You can also set "Preferred brand", "Preferred origin" and
"Prefer verified organic products". Click "Save preferences".

Reload and check that the saved values return in the same buyer workspace.
Start "New request". "Use saved optional preferences for this new request" controls
whether those soft defaults are applied. Explicit request instructions and
hard dietary, origin, brand and budget requirements take priority.

Saving optional brand/origin/organic/priority defaults must not rewrite a cart
already under review. Saving mandatory food requirements does recheck the
current unconfirmed cart and can block products that no longer meet them.
Use "Other excluded ingredients" for up to eight specific exclusions. There
is no general note editor promising unsupported automatic preferences.
"Clear saved preferences" resets the saved defaults; it does not delete orders.

To check isolation, sign into a different private workspace. It must not load
the first buyer's preferences or saved-cart pointer. Sign back into the first
workspace to recover its own records. The shared demonstration is intentionally
shared by people using the same demonstration code.



5. CREATE AND RESOLVE A BUDGET CONFLICT

In the shopping cart, enter a deliberately low value such as 1 in
"Maximum budget (EUR )". Click "Apply requirements".

Expected result:

  - The budget balance shows that the selected items exceed the budget.
  - The reason is explained.
  - "Review & confirm" is unavailable.
  - The application does not quietly remove items or increase the budget.

Correct the budget by entering an amount above the displayed item subtotal,
or remove the optional budget value. Click "Apply requirements" again.
Alternatively, reduce quantities or choose cheaper compliant products.

The hard customer budget and the buyer's purchasing authority are different:
raising your budget does not grant the buyer more approval authority.

"Item subtotal" includes quoted item prices and tax. Delivery and service
charges are excluded from this simulation. It is not a final commercial bill.
A partial cart must clearly say that requests still need attention.


6. CHECK DELIVERY AND EXPIRED QUOTATIONS

Check "Requested delivery" in the cart. Choose a valid date and click
"Apply requirements". Delivery estimates must relate to that date.

Use a date from today through the next 30 days. On 14 November 2026, the next
Monday is 16 November 2026. On later test days, choose an appropriate current
date instead of reusing an expired example.

A missing or invalid date, unavailable stock or unsupported delivery date
should keep the full cart from being confirmed.

A quotation is short-lived. Leave an otherwise ready cart open until its
shown validity time passes. It should show that the quote expired and prevent
approval or confirmation. Click "Refresh prices & availability", then review
the current quotation. Refreshing creates a fresh decision; previous exact
approval must not automatically apply to changed facts.

For a faster controlled stale-offer example, open "Trust scenarios", choose
"The offer changes", click "Send to buyer agent", and use
"Refresh this exact offer".
The comparison should explain what changed.


7. TEST THE 500-DINER MEAL REQUEST

In "Smart order", click "New request" and choose "Bolognese for 500 diners".
Its request includes 20% vegetarian diners, 10% gluten-free diners and an
overlap of 20 diners who need both. Click "Build cart".

Expected result:

  - The meal expands into ingredient requests with quantities.
  - The proposed recipe is labelled as illustrative and needs chef review.
  - Gluten-free and vegetarian requirements remain visible where required.
  - Unsupported products or dietary evidence produce honest conflicts.
  - The application does not claim the complete meal can be purchased merely
    because some ingredients matched.

Review the recipe and click "I have reviewed the proposed recipe" only after
you have actually reviewed it. This records a review; it is not a food-safety
certification. Unresolved product requirements must still block confirmation.

Also try a request that mentions vegetarian and gluten-free percentages but
omits the overlap. The assistant should ask how many diners need both, rather
than assume an overlap silently. In "Diners who need both diets", enter 20 and
click "Apply overlap". Enter 0 only if the groups do not overlap. Check that the
question clears and the ingredient requirements are recalculated. If you
provided a valid overlap already, it should not ask the same question again.

This direct overlap correction was observed in the local browser: the question
cleared and the saved ingredient requests changed from 10 to 11. That is a
recalculation result, not a claim that all dietary products became available.

In "Quote shopping plan":

"Export draft shopping plan (JSON)" remains a draft export. Exporting the

9. CHECK SEPARATE MANAGER APPROVAL

Run this once in the fixture, after the smaller tests. Repeated confirmed
orders consume that buyer workspace's simulated spending allowance.

Start "New request" as buyer and enter:

  I need 1500 litres of lactose-free milk delivered by Monday.
  Choose the cheapest suitable product.

With the default demonstration policy and fixture SKU 464306, the expected
item subtotal is EUR 1,725.00. Check the selected SKU and displayed price; do not
assume that a different product or live quotation has this same amount.

The default autonomous limit is EUR 1,400.00 and the exception ceiling is
EUR 2,500.00. The cart should therefore show "Purchasing approval needed", and
the buyer must not be able to approve it personally.

Click "Sign in as approver". Enter the separate approver code in the sign-in
window and click "Open this session". Review the exact product, quantity,
price, date and remaining issues, then click "Approve this exact cart".

Switch back to the buyer identity using the profile button and buyer code.
The same saved cart should reopen. It should show that this exact cart has
manager approval. Only the buyer can continue to confirmation.

Before confirming, optionally change the quantity or date. The old approval
must be cleared because the approved cart has changed. Obtain fresh approval
for the new exact cart if its value still requires it.

For a private registered workspace, open your profile and click
"Approve this workspace". It pre-fills "Manager workspace ID (optional)" in
"Demo access". Enter the separate approver code and click "Open this session".
Review and approve the exact cart in that workspace, then return through
"Account sign-in" with the buyer's username and password to confirm.

If the quote expires during the handoff, approval must stay blocked. Return
as buyer, click "Refresh prices & availability", review the fresh exact cart,
then return as manager for a new approval. The manager cannot refresh or edit
the buyer's requirements merely to bypass expiry. This expiry -> refresh ->
exact approval -> buyer confirmation -> receipt verification sequence passed
the recorded local browser check for a EUR 1,725 private cart.

A workspace ID alone grants no permissions, and entering the buyer code must
not turn the buyer into an approver. Leave the manager workspace field blank
only when intentionally returning to the shared demonstration.

Hard-rule failures are different from budget approval. A manager must not be
able to approve missing stock, an invalid conversion, a hard dietary failure
or an exceeded hard customer budget.

The cumulative cap is EUR 6,000.00 by default in each buyer workspace. The
shared demonstration is one such workspace. If earlier checks used its cap
up, ask the owner to prepare a clean demonstration ledger; do not expect a
buyer or approver button to bypass it. Operator settings may change these
demonstration thresholds.


10. CONFIRM AND VERIFY A SIMULATED RECEIPT

Use a complete, permitted cart with any required approval obtained.

Click "Review & confirm". Review the products, item subtotal and requested
date. Then click "Confirm simulated order".

Expected result: "Simulated order recorded." appears with an actual generated
reference. The main workspace also shows "Your order, on the record." with the
saved item list, exact sales-package quantities and planned quantity/surplus,
requested delivery date, item subtotal, source and manager-approval record.
Check these against the cart you reviewed.

"What happens next?" explains that the simulation stops here. No supplier
delivery is scheduled and no supplier order, invoice or payment is created.
The requested date is a recorded request, not a delivery booking. An actual
purchase would need your authorized supplier ordering channel and contract.

Click "Verify receipt". It should report that the saved receipt matches the
recorded order. This is a read-only check: it should not create another order
or consume more of the simulated spending allowance. You can use
"Verify receipt again" to repeat that check.

Reload the browser. The saved cart and its simulated receipt should reopen
after sign-in if necessary. The reference should be unchanged. A receipt is
not an invoice, a supplier confirmation or a guarantee of delivery.


11. OPTIONAL OPERATOR CHECKS: TEXT INTERPRETER AND PRICE SOURCE

These are optional technical checks, separate from ordinary ordering. Open
"Demo tools" -> "Data & model settings", start a new request, then open
"Demo settings". "Quotation source" offers "Live sponsor sandbox" or
"Authored fixture prices & stock". Live mode reads challenge sandbox price
and availability; confirmation remains our simulation. Existing saved carts
keep their original source.

The text-interpreter checkbox "Use NVIDIA for nonconfidential demo requests"
is independent of the price/stock source. Typed-request interpretation defaults
to the local parser, with this operator checkbox unchecked.
This operator setting is for interpreting text requests. Uploaded images and
camera photos use the automatic image reader described in section 13B instead.
You can deliberately choose any of these combinations:

  Rehearsal fixture + NVIDIA off: local parser, authored prices and stock.
  Rehearsal fixture + NVIDIA on: hosted interpretation, authored prices/stock.
  Live sandbox + NVIDIA off: local parser, live sandbox prices and stock.
  Live sandbox + NVIDIA on: hosted interpretation and live sandbox lookups.

Checking NVIDIA can make a hosted model request even with fixture prices.
Some supported recipe turns use the local deterministic recipe planner. Do not
assume that every result used NVIDIA simply because the checkbox was selected.
Model-routing verification belongs in the technical evidence report; the
ordinary response banner omits internal routing labels.

Look for clear source labels. The fixture must never be presented as live.
If a live service times out or returns an error, the application should show
the error and preserve the existing saved cart. It must not quietly substitute
successful-looking fixture data. Hosted services can be temporarily unavailable;
record the error separately from a failure of the local rehearsal workflow.

Start another "New request" to change an existing cart's source or interpreter.
A source change should not silently alter an already reviewed quotation.


12. CHECK HARD FAILURES IN THE TRUST SCENARIOS

"Trust scenarios" preserves the focused shared milk-order demonstration. A

13A. TYPE OR RECORD A VOICE REQUEST

In Smart order, "Type a message" focuses the request field. For speech,
click "Voice input". While permission is pending, look for
"Allow microphone access in your browser..." and "Cancel". Repeated activation
must not start multiple recordings. Cancel must release the ordering controls;
a late permission response must not silently begin recording.

Allow microphone access only when
you intend to record. Say a short demonstration request such as:

  I need twenty litres of lactose-free milk and five kilograms of butter
  delivered by Monday.

Click "Stop & transcribe". The recording automatically stops after 30 seconds.
"Cancel" discards the current recording. After stopping normally,
the clip is sent to this application's server for local Whisper transcription.
It is not sent to an external speech provider by the default implementation.

Inspect "Review extracted text" and "Editable extracted text".
Correct product names, numbers, units and dietary wording. Read any uncertainty
messages. Click "Add to request" only after review. It adds editable
text to the request; it must not build or confirm a cart automatically.

During recording, extraction and unreviewed extracted text, "Build cart" or
"Update cart" stays unavailable. While reviewing text, "Voice input", "Upload
image" and "Take a photo" also stay unavailable so they cannot replace it.
Typing and editing stay available. Use "Add to request", or the close button
labelled "Discard extracted text", to finish that review and release ordering.

You can combine it with typed text, then click "Build cart" or "Update cart"
yourself. There is no audio-file upload control. The microphone still uses
the retained audio-processing API. A declined microphone permission must give
a useful message and leave typing, image upload and photo capture available.


13B. READ A PHOTO OR PRINTED SHOPPING LIST

Click "Upload image" and choose one clear JPEG or PNG of a nonconfidential
example shopping list. The image reader is selected automatically; there is
no provider selector or per-image checkbox. "Input privacy" explains that an
external service may process images. Use only nonconfidential demonstration
content; do not upload credentials, customer names, contracts or private
Valio Aimo material.

The original file must be nonempty and no larger than 5 MB. Large-resolution
phone images within that byte limit are resized proportionally in the browser
to at most 1600 pixels on the longest side, with camera orientation respected.
A fresh image removes the original filename and metadata before upload. The
server keeps its independent image-validation defenses for direct requests.

Check the editable extracted text against the image. Fix uncertain characters,
quantities and units before "Add to request". A product photo
without an explicit purchasing quantity must not cause an invented quantity.
A blank, corrupt or unsupported file should explain the problem, not create
an order.

Try keeping a short typed request in the composer before uploading the image.
While "Reading your shopping list..." is shown, its text must remain intact
and Build/Update must be unavailable. The same guard remains while extracted
text awaits Add or Discard; choosing Add combines it with the existing request.

Image reading uses the configured automatic vision service; a temporary
provider failure may use the local OCR fallback. No customer model decision
is required. Uploaded media is processed for text extraction, not saved as a
workspace attachment. Text you choose to add becomes part of the purchasing
request only when you submit it. Review names, quantities and units yourself;
image reading does not establish prices, inventory or dietary safety.


13C. TAKE A PHOTO

Click "Take a photo". On a supported phone, use the native camera. On desktop,
allow camera access, inspect the preview and click "Capture photo". While
waiting for permission, the app says "Allow camera access in your browser to
continue." "Cancel" and the close button must release the pending state.
With keyboard focus inside the camera preview, Escape should close it too.
Closing, leaving the screen or unmounting must stop any camera stream, including
a late permission grant. A late photo callback after Cancel must not upload it.

Read and correct the same "Editable extracted text" used for uploaded images,
then explicitly "Add to request". Capture must not build or confirm a cart.
Declining permission or having no camera must give useful recovery through
"Upload image" or typing. Photos use the same automatic image reader and
proportional resizing as uploaded images. The 5 MB original-file limit still
applies; a phone photo must not be rejected merely because its original pixel
count exceeds the server's direct-request limit.

The owner confirmed the physical phone/photo flow on the final public release:

automated camera checks separately use fake streams and canvas callbacks.
Neither a single owner retest nor those unit checks establishes support or
accuracy for every phone, webcam, browser, permission dialog or handwriting.

15. CURRENT LIMITATIONS TO KEEP IN MIND

This is a procurement prototype using restricted challenge data and synthetic
customer identities. The local parser supports structured English shopping
requests and a specific bolognese example; it is not an unrestricted expert on
every dish, language or purchasing specification.

Recipes are illustrative until reviewed by the kitchen. Declared dietary
claims do not establish complete allergy safety or safe preparation. Lactose-
free milk can still contain milk allergens. Missing or contradictory product
facts should prevent automatic acceptance rather than be invented.

The quotation covers tax-inclusive items. Shipping/service charges, real
customer contracts, credit checks, invoices, payments and real supplier order
submission are not integrated. Historical delivered-quantity reliability is
not an on-time-delivery guarantee. Public product listing is not proof of
current orderable stock.

Live service availability and prices may change. Fixture prices with the local
typed-request parser provide a repeatable check of deterministic validation.
The public-site microphone worked correctly in one user-reported accent test.
Wider voice accuracy, noisy kitchens and real camera/handwriting coverage still
need representative checks; a clean synthetic benchmark does not establish it. The
workspace's simulated spending allowance and policy expiry can affect later tests.

No final Junction project submission is performed by these website checks.

Historical persistence check: 13 checks passed after the earlier redeployment.
The same remembered session, account login, preferences, simulated spending and
three saved receipts/approval records recovered. A private backup passed SQLite
integrity; this check created no new orders.
